# Product Vision

## Mission
Empower local retailers to sell online while delivering fast, trusted grocery experiences to customers.

## Vision
Build India’s largest local-first commerce platform by digitizing kirana stores and enabling hyperlocal economies.

## Target Users
- Customers in Tier-2 & Tier-3 cities
- Local grocery & specialty retailers
- Delivery partners

## Core Principles
- Retailer empowerment
- Fast settlements
- Low commissions
- Reliable delivery
- Scalable technology
